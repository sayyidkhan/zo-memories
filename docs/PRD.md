# Zo Moments

> A shared digital home for the moments that matter.

Version: v0.1

---

## Vision

Zo Moments is a shared digital memory space where two or more people can preserve, organise, and rediscover life's moments together.

Instead of memories being scattered across phones, messaging apps, and cloud providers, Zo Moments gives every relationship a single place to grow.

Powered by Zo Storage, every photo, video, voice note, and document becomes part of a shared story.

## One Liner

> A shared digital home where people preserve, organise, and rediscover life's moments together.

## Problem Statement

Today's memories live everywhere:

- WhatsApp
- Telegram
- Google Photos
- iCloud
- Laptops
- External hard drives

As years pass, memories become fragmented. People lose photos, videos disappear, voice notes are forgotten, and important documents become impossible to find.

Cloud storage solves file storage. It does not preserve relationships.

---

## Solution

Zo Moments creates a shared digital space where people collect memories together.

Every upload becomes part of a living timeline. AI helps users rediscover moments instead of simply storing files.

## Core Features

### Shared Spaces

Users create a private shared space.

Examples:

- Sayyid & Sarah
- Khan Family
- Prompt Alchemist
- Apartment 12

Every space has its own memories.

### Upload Memories

Supported content:

- Photos
- Videos
- Voice notes
- PDFs
- Documents

### Timeline

Uploads are displayed chronologically.

```text
June 2026
  |
Tokyo Trip
  |
143 Photos
  |
12 Videos
  |
2 Voice Notes
```

### Albums

Users can organise memories into albums, such as:

- Vacation
- Wedding
- Birthday
- Graduation

### Stories

Users turn a set of moments into a story with a title, place, personal opening, and chronological reading experience. Stories are the primary way to revisit an event; albums remain optional organisation.

The creation journey is explicit inside every shared space:

1. Create a shared space.
2. Invite the people who were there.
3. Add moments together.
4. Shape selected moments into a story.

### Search

Search by:

- Filename
- Date
- Album

Future: natural-language search.

### Preview

- Images
- Videos
- Audio
- PDFs

### Shared Uploads

Every member of a space can upload memories.

### Shareable Invitations

Space owners create a single-use invitation link and share it through WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, the device share sheet, or any other messaging app. Links expire after 30 days and can be replaced or revoked by the owner.

Recipients can preview the space name, then create an account or sign in before joining.

### AI Memory Timeline (Future)

AI automatically creates:

- Monthly recaps
- Trip summaries
- Memory highlights
- Stories

### AI Search (Future)

Examples:

- "Show every beach trip."
- "When did we first visit Japan?"
- "Find grandma's birthday."
